Having Trouble Logging In?

I forgot my password. What should I do?
Click the "Forgot Password?" link below the login form. Enter your registered email address, and we'll send you a secure reset link. Check your spam/junk folder if you don't see it within 5 minutes.
My account is suspended. How do I fix it?
If your account is suspended, please contact our customer support via Telegram (@Nexusearncustomerservice) or WhatsApp (link in footer). Our team will assist you within 24 hours.
Can I login with my phone number?
Yes! You can login using your 11‑digit phone number (e.g., 080XXXXXXXX), your email address, or your username. The system will automatically detect which one you entered.
I'm getting "Too many attempts". What now?
For security, we limit login attempts. Wait 5‑10 minutes before trying again. If you've forgotten your password, use the reset link instead of repeatedly guessing.
