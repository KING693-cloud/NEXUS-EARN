Having Trouble?

I didn't receive the reset email. What should I do?
First, check your Spam or Junk folder. If it's not there, wait 5 minutes and try again. Ensure you entered the correct email address. Still not received? Contact support via Telegram.
The reset link expired. Can I get a new one?
Yes! Simply go back to this page and request a new reset link. The link is valid for 1 hour from the time it's sent.
I forgot which email I used to register.
Try logging in with your username or phone number on the login page. If you're still unable, contact our support team with your phone number or username for assistance.
Can I reset my password via phone number?
Currently, password reset is available only via email. If you don't have access to your email, please contact support for manual identity verification.

Still stuck? Our support team is available 24/7 via Telegram @Nexusearncustomerservice and WhatsApp (link in footer). We're here to help.
