Deposit FAQs

How do I deposit?
Simply transfer the desired amount to the bank account displayed above. Use the auto‑generated narration code as your transaction narration. Then, fill in your name, account number, and amount on this page and submit. Our admin will verify and credit your balance within 24‑48 hours. Minimum deposit is ₦15,000.
Why do I need a narration code?
The narration code helps us match your payment instantly. Without it, verification may take longer. Always copy and use the exact code shown.
How long does it take for my balance to update?
After you submit the request, our admin will verify the transaction. This usually takes 24‑48 hours. Once verified, your balance will be updated instantly.
Is there a minimum deposit amount?
Yes, the minimum deposit is ₦15,000. There is no maximum limit.
