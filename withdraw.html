Withdrawal FAQs

Why is there a 10% processing fee?
The 10% fee covers transaction costs, bank processing, and administrative verification. It ensures that all withdrawals are handled securely and efficiently. You receive 90% of your requested amount.
How long does a withdrawal take?
Withdrawals are processed manually by admin for security. Please allow 24‑48 hours for approval. You'll see the status update in your History page. Requests made outside working hours (Mon–Sat, 10‑6 WAT) are processed the next business day.
Why can't I withdraw if I have a pending request?
To prevent double‑processing and ensure security, we allow only one pending withdrawal request at a time. Once your current request is approved or rejected, you can submit a new one – even on the same day.
My withdrawal was rejected. What now?
If your withdrawal is rejected, a reason will be provided in the History page. Common reasons: incorrect bank details, insufficient balance, or narration mismatch. Update your bank details in the Profile tab and try again.
