STEP 1 Download the App
Get the NEXUS EARN app for faster access and instant notifications.
  • Android: Tap the floating Download App button (bottom‑left) or the "Get Nexus App" button inside the Voucher modal.
  • iOS: Open Safari, go to nexus-earn.com, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Once installed, you'll get real‑time updates and a smoother experience.
STEP 2 Deposit via Bank Transfer
Fund your wallet using our secure PalmPay account.
  • Bank: PalmPay
  • Account Number: 896-2925-124
  • Account Name: NEXUS EARN LIMITED(TAIWO)
  • ⚠️ On OPay, it may show as NEXUS(TAIWO) – that's still correct.
  • Minimum Deposit: ₦15,000
  • Go to the Deposit page, generate your 10‑character narration code, include it in your transfer, and submit the form.
  • Admin approves within 24 hours – 0% fee – you get exactly what you sent.
STEP 3 Earn Daily Returns
After activating a VIP plan, you earn daily for 365 days.
  • Go to the Mining tab (bottom navigation).
  • Tap the collect income button once per day.
  • Your earnings are added to your balance instantly.
  • You must collect every day – missed days are lost.
  • Upgrade to a higher VIP plan to increase your daily yield.
STEP 4 Withdraw Your Earnings
Cash out your earnings securely to your bank account.
  • Minimum: ₦500
  • Processing Fee: 10% (you receive 90%)
  • Hours: Mon – Sat, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM (WAT)
  • No withdrawals on Sundays.
  • Only one pending withdrawal at a time – wait for approval before requesting another.
  • Admin processes within 24‑48 hours.
