How It Works

Follow these steps to start earning in minutes.

STEP 1 Deposit via Bank Transfer
Fund your wallet to activate a VIP plan.
  • Minimum Deposit: ₦15,000
  • Go to the Deposit page from your dashboard.
  • Copy the PalmPay account details shown.
  • Generate your 10‑character narration code and include it in your transfer.
  • Submit the deposit form with your sender name.
  • Admin approves within 24 hours – 0% fee.
STEP 2 Activate a VIP Plan
Use your deposited funds to purchase a 365‑day investment plan.
  • On the Home tab, scroll to the VIP plans list.
  • Choose from VIP 1 – VIP 13.
  • Each plan pays a fixed daily return for 365 days.
  • Tap "Activate Plan" – funds are deducted instantly.
  • Example: VIP 1 costs ₦15,500 → ₦500/day → ₦182,500 total.
STEP 3 Claim Daily Earnings
Collect your daily returns once every 24 hours.
  • Go to the Mining tab (bottom navigation).
  • Your daily yield is shown at the top.
  • Tap the large collect button once per day.
  • Earnings are added to your balance instantly.
  • The button resets at midnight (Nigeria time).
  • ⚠️ Missed days are NOT carried over – collect daily!
STEP 4 Withdraw Your Earnings
Cash out your earnings to your bank account.
  • Minimum: ₦500
  • Processing Fee: 10% (you receive 90%)
  • Hours: Mon – Sat, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM (WAT)
  • No withdrawals on Sundays.
  • Only one pending withdrawal at a time.
  • Admin processes within 24‑48 hours.
  • You must have a linked bank account in your Profile tab.
💡 Refer Friends & Earn Commission
Share your unique referral link and earn passive income.
  • Your referral link is in the Team tab.
  • When a friend registers with your link and activates a VIP plan, you earn 10% commission.
  • Example: Friend buys VIP 1 (₦15,500) → you earn ₦1,550.
  • Earn milestone bonuses as your team's total deposits grow.
  • Track your team and bonuses in the Team tab.
